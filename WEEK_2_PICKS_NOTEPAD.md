## 📊 How to Grade the Model on Sunday Morning

1. **Record Final Scores:** On Sunday morning, input the official final scores in the `Final Score` column.
2. **Grade Each Bet:**
   * **Favorite Bets (e.g. `BYU -5.0` or `Miami -25.5`):** Win if the favorite wins by MORE than the spread margin.
   * **Underdog Bets (e.g. `Villanova +19.0`, `Kansas +15.5`, or `Arkansas +23.0`):** Win if the underdog wins outright OR loses by LESS than the spread points.
   * **Push:** If margin matches the spread exactly, it is a push (refund/neutral).
   * **Pass:** Games marked `PASS (Fair Market Line)` (e.g. Ohio State at Texas) do not count toward ATS grading.
3. **Key ATS Grading Targets:**
   * **52.4% ATS:** Standard sports betting break-even hurdle at -110 juice.
   * **55.0% - 58.0% ATS:** Elite handicapper benchmark (sustained profit).
   * **60.0%+ ATS:** God tier predictive accuracy.
