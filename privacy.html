Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CFB Prophet ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CFB Prophet iOS application and web simulation platform.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, track, or sell your personal data.

  • No Tracking: CFB Prophet does not use tracking identifiers, advertising SDKs, or data brokers.
  • Local Storage: Your custom game simulations, slider settings, and bracket projections are stored securely on your local device (localStorage / App Container).
  • Account Sign-In (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google or create a coach profile, your authentication token is verified securely via Supabase Auth solely to sync your saved predictions across your devices. We do not use your email for marketing or spam.

3. Cloud Sync & Community Submissions

When you explicitly choose to submit a bracket to the public Community Leaderboard or scan a QR code, only the game pick predictions, accuracy score, and your chosen display name are broadcast. No private personal data is ever shared.

4. Children's Privacy

CFB Prophet is suitable for all audiences (rated 4+) and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may display team logos and schedules provided via public athletic feeds solely for simulation reference under fair-use editorial guidelines.
