Privacy Policy

CFB Prophet was built for college football fans to simulate games and test playoff scenarios. We do not track you, sell your data, run third-party advertising networks, or harvest personal info.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

By default, all of your simulated games, custom score overrides, and team slider adjustments are stored directly in your web browser or device storage (via local storage).

This data belongs to you and remains on your device until you clear your browser data or delete the app.

2. Community Brackets & Public Leaderboard

If you choose to submit a bracket to the public Community Leaderboard, the app sends your game picks, predicted scores, and the display name or nickname you entered to our database.

These submissions are public so other users can view and compare picks. Please do not submit sensitive personal information (such as your real full name, phone number, or home address) as your bracket name or handle.

3. Account Sign-In (Optional)

Creating an account or signing in with Google or an email link is completely optional. If you choose to sign in, we only store your authentication record to link your saved predictions to your profile across your devices.

We will never sell your email, spam you, or share your account info with data brokers or marketing firms.

4. Live Sports Feeds

CFB Prophet pulls weekly game schedules, team ratings, and live scores from public athletic APIs (such as ESPN). We do not send your personal identity or browsing habits to these providers.

5. Analytics & Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party ad pixels. The app only stores functional preferences (such as your active team theme and saved predictions) necessary for the simulator to run.
