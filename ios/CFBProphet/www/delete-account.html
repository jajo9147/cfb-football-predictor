Account & Data Deletion

We believe you should have complete ownership and control over your personal data. Below are the steps to immediately and permanently delete your CFB Prophet account and all associated prediction records.

OPTION 1 • INSTANT IN-APP DELETION

Delete Directly from the CFB Prophet App

1. Open CFB Prophet on your iPhone, iPad, or browser.

2. Tap your Profile Name / Icon in the top-right navigation bar.

3. Scroll to the bottom of the profile dialog and tap "Delete Account & Prediction Data".

4. On the confirmation prompt, tap "Yes, Permanently Delete".

5. Your account, saved brackets, custom simulation settings, and database entries will be erased immediately, and you will be signed out.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All personal playoff projections, weekly pick records, and community bracket references tied to your user ID will be permanently purged.

OPTION 2 • EMAIL REQUEST

Submit a Manual Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer assistance, you may email our support team at jajo9147@gmail.com with your registered email address or username. All associated records will be permanently purged from all systems within 24 hours.

For additional information regarding our data privacy practices, please review our Privacy Policy.
