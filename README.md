## ⚡ Key Capabilities

- **Dynamic School Themes**: Real-time switching of broadcast colors, stadium glows, and branding for all 10 powerhouses.
- **10,000 Monte Carlo Drives Engine**: Poisson/EPA-calibrated drive simulator generating real-time scoring, field goal attempts, and turnover logs.
- **Single-Game AI Scenario Builder**: Custom tune individual matchups (QB slumps, blowout traps, turnover variance) overriding season baselines.
- **12-Team CFP Playoff Gauntlet**: Dynamically positions First Round hosts, Quarterfinal bowl seeds, and National Championship paths.
- **Social Hype Card Generator**: Canvas-rendered social cards ready to copy/paste into group chats.
- **Progressive Web App ($0 Install)**: 1-tap "Add to Home Screen" on iOS & Android for a fullscreen standalone app experience.
